Central Market of São Luís

Opened in 1941 in Art Deco style, the Central Market is where São Luís buys its fish, fruit, spices and crafts. The 1941 building was demolished in September 2026 and a new one is being built; meanwhile, the stalls work at the Mercado da Cidade.

This page is an automatic translation that is still being reviewed. The full site (book and interactive map) is in Portuguese.

Where it is

Historic building: Rua de São João, Centro, next to the Fonte das Pedras. During the works: Mercado da Cidade, Av. Senador Vitorino Freire, Centro.

When to go

Stalls open early, around 5 a.m. The first hours of the morning have the widest choice of fish and fruit.

What you will find

Fresh fish and shrimp, dried shrimp, juçara (açaí), bacuri, cupuaçu, tiquira, cassava flour, spices, cofo baskets and local crafts.

By bus

“Mercado Central” stop, Travessa do Mercado. Lines: 302, T005, T031, T032, T033, T034, T036. The Praia Grande Integration Terminal (Av. Senador Vitorino Freire) receives lines from almost every neighbourhood. Lines may change: check at the stop.

Getting around

The historic centre (Praia Grande) is a short walk away: Fonte das Pedras, Rua Portugal, Teatro Arthur Azevedo, Palácio dos Leões. The beaches (Calhau, Olho d’Água) are about 25–35 minutes by car.
